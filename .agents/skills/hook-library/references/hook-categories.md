# Hook Categories A–Z

The 26 distinct angles from `req.txt`. Use this table to check coverage before finalising a hook set. Each row gives the technique, the psychological lever, and a shape — not a line to copy verbatim.

| # | Category | Technique | Psychological lever | Example shape |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Curiosity | Create an information gap; challenge an assumption | "I need to know what happens next" | "There's something about [place] that most people miss." |
| B | Question | Ask a relatable, thought-provoking question | Automatic answer reflex | "When did you last [ordinary thing] without [expectation]?" |
| C | Problem-Based | Name an everyday frustration or rarely discussed struggle | Recognition and validation | "The worst part of [task] isn't [obvious thing]." |
| D | Relatable | Start with a daily situation people recognise | "This happens to me too" | "Have you ever noticed that…" |
| E | Contrarian | Challenge a popular opinion without being offensive | Pattern interrupt + mild disagreement | "Maybe the problem isn't what you think it is." |
| F | Storytelling | Start in the middle of an incident; add tension | Narrative suspense | "She walked in, saw [unexpected thing], and stayed." |
| G | Emotional | Trigger belonging, nostalgia, empathy, or warmth | Feeling understood | "Sometimes, all we need is someone to [small act]." |
| H | Shock / Pattern-Interrupt | Surprising but true observation; break the predictable opening | Attention reset | "Nobody talks about the [unexpected object] in [ordinary place]." |
| I | Direct-Address | Speak to one specific viewer | Personal relevance | "If you're someone who [behaviour], this is for you." |
| J | Challenge | Invite a test or friendly competition | Participation impulse | "Try this for one day and tell me what happens." |
| K | Confession | Honest admission of a common mistake | Authenticity and trust | "I used to think [belief]. I was wrong." |
| L | Comparison | This vs that, before vs after | Contrast makes the point fast | "[A] feels like [X]. [B] feels like [Y]." |
| M | Myth-Busting | Separate assumption from reality | Correction reflex | "Everyone thinks [assumption]. Here's what actually happens." |
| N | Community-Identity | Make viewers feel part of a group | Belonging | "This is what people around here do when [situation]." |
| O | Observation | Point out an unnoticed everyday pattern | "I've seen that too" | "There's a person who [small repeated action] every [day]." |
| P | FOMO | Highlight a real missed opportunity — never fake urgency | Regret avoidance | "A lot of people still haven't tried [real thing]." |
| Q | Humour | Everyday comedy, awkward moments, light sarcasm | Amusement | "The most [adjective] thing about [topic] is [specific detail]." |
| R | Data / Fact | One verified, interesting fact and why it matters | Credibility + surprise | "One verified fact: [fact]. Here's why it matters here." |
| S | Scenario | "Imagine if…" — make viewers visualise | Mental simulation | "Imagine if [place] had [small change]." |
| T | List-Based | "3 things nobody tells you about…" | Structured curiosity | "3 things nobody tells you about [topic]." |
| U | Negative | What to stop doing; real problem, no fearmongering | Loss aversion | "Stop [common behaviour] if you want [genuine result]." |
| V | Insider / Secret | Overlooked practical insight, not fake secrecy | Exclusivity + utility | "One thing people don't notice about [topic] is…" |
| W | Social-Proof | Real participation or genuine member story only | "People like me are doing this" | "Last week, someone actually [real action]." |
| X | Future-Vision | Paint a picture of a better community | Hope and agency | "Imagine a [place] where [better behaviour] was normal." |
| Y | Interactive | Poll, either/or, complete the sentence | Choice reflex | "Let's settle this once and for all: [A] or [B]?" |
| Z | Local / Neighbourhood | Local problems, places, and people | Shared location identity | "If you live near [place], you've probably noticed [thing]." |

## Hard rules

- A fact hook (R) needs a reliable source. If none is available, switch category.
- Social proof (W) must be real. Never invent a testimonial, member, or result.
- FOMO (P) must not manufacture urgency or scarcity.
- Shock (H) and contrarian (E) must not target or trivialise vulnerable people.
- Coverage target: at least 12 of the 26 categories in a 30-hook set.
