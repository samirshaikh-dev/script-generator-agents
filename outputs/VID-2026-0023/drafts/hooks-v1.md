# Hook Library & Strategy — VID-2026-0023

## 1. Strategy & Brief
- **Campaign / Request ID:** VID-2026-0023
- **Title:** New City, No Friends
- **Objective:** Drive Community Connection & Trust (Objective 6 — Community Connection & Objective 2 — Build Trust) + Feature Education (Objective 1).
- **Target Audience:** Relocated young professionals, newcomers, college freshers, remote workers, and young women (20–32) living in metro hubs like Bengaluru, Ranchi, or Gaya.
- **Participation Ladder:**
  - *Viewer:* Resonates with the silent ache of urban loneliness and eating meals alone in a new city.
  - *Participant:* Opens meelTe, explores the Interests tab, connects with nearby hobby peers, and shares location to meet up.
  - *Regular Member:* Forms genuine, recurring friend circles, transforming an alien city into a welcoming home.
- **Tone & Voice:** Empathetic, emotional, cinematic, comforting, triumphant.

## 2. Recommended Hook
- **Hook ID:** H13 (Category G — Emotional / User Verbatim)
- **Script line:** *A girl eats alone in a new city. Text: "Day 12 in Bengaluru."*
- **Visual:** Young woman sitting alone at a small cafe window table, eating silently while watching groups laughing around her.
- **Psychological lever:** Visceral urban loneliness empathy, relatable migrant vulnerability, desire for genuine belonging.
