## 1. Video objective

**Drive Community Connection & Build Trust (Objective 6 — Community Connection & Objective 2 — Build Trust) and Promote meelTe App Features (Objective 1).** Address the profound emotional struggle of young professionals and students who relocate to major hubs like Bengaluru and experience isolating loneliness. Showcase meelTe's "Interests" discovery and in-chat location sharing (Clip I), showing that genuine, shared-hobby friendships can be formed in days rather than months.

---

## 2. Target audience

Relocated young professionals, newcomers, college students, remote workers, and young women (20–32) living in metro cities who want authentic, offline friendships based on real shared passions.
