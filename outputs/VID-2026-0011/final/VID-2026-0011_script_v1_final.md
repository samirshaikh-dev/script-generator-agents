## 1. Video objective

**Build Connection, Build Trust, Build Relationships & Drive Participation.** Bridge the generational gap in local neighbourhood commerce. Show that traditional, decades-old family stores do not need to feel left behind in the digital age. By partnering with younger family members to post on meelTe, beloved legacy shopkeepers gain modern visibility, inviting the under-25 generation into their shops.

---

## 2. Target audience

Senior and legacy shop owners running heritage neighbourhood businesses (kirana, sweets, general merchants, cloth stores), younger family members who want to help their parents or grandparents, and neighbourhood youth looking for authentic local shops.
