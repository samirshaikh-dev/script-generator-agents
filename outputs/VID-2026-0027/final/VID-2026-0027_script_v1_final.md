## 1. Executive Summary & Brief

### Community Building Objective & Rationale
To transform the passive mindset of "finding local shops takes too long" into an active, gamified community challenge. By demonstrating that any neighborhood resident can locate three essential walking-distance shops (bakery, tailor, chemist) in under 25 seconds using meelTe's category and distance filters, this video builds proof of local convenience and invites viewers to test and share their own local discovery speeds.

### Participation Ladder
1. **Viewer**: Watches the high-energy 30-second speedrun challenge and witnesses the countdown stop at 24.12s.
2. **Participant**: Opens the meelTe app, applies the 500m distance filter, and finds 3 shops in their own neighborhood.
3. **Regular Member**: Bookmarks walking-distance neighborhood shops and turns to meelTe whenever an immediate neighborhood need arises.
4. **Community Advocate**: Posts their screen-recording speed or comments their record time, sparking friendly neighborhood banter.
