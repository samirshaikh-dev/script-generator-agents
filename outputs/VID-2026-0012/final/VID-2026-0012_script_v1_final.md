## 1. Video objective

**Drive Participation, Create Awareness & Build Trust.** Overcome the common merchant hesitation that creating digital advertisements is difficult, time-consuming, or requires agency support. By showing the full meelTe Create Advertisement process completed against a live stopwatch in under two minutes (1:58), local retailers are empowered to post time-sensitive deals effortlessly.

---

## 2. Target audience

Busy local shopkeepers (grocery, retail, apparel, electronics, stationery) who want to promote sales but lack time, and neighbourhood shoppers searching for limited-time local offers.

---

## 3. Hook (0–3s) — selected verbatim

A stopwatch starts. A shop owner picks up his phone.

**Rohit:** "Chalo, dekhte hain 2 minute mein ad banta hai ya nahi!"
