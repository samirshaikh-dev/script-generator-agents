## 1. Video objective

**Create Awareness (Objective 4), Build Trust (Objective 2), and Drive Participation (Objective 5).** Highlight the universal consumer frustration with anonymous online shopping: wrong items delivered, return logistics delays, and photos that don't match reality. Contrast delivery lottery gambles with meelTe's verified local discovery where buyers see what nearby stores actually have in stock with real photos, clear prices, and short distances—inspecting products in person with zero return headaches.

---

## 2. Target audience

Young urban professionals, students, and tech enthusiasts (18–35) exhausted by e-commerce delivery mistakes and return logistics, as well as neighbourhood retail store owners stocking genuine merchandise.
