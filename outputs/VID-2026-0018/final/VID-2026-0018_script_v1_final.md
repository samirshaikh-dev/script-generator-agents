## 1. Video objective

**Drive Local Discovery & Direct Walk-In Footfall (Objective 5 — Drive Participation), Promote meelTe App Features (Objective 1), and Build Trust (Objective 2).** Tap into the universal, high-stakes relatability of an Indian household errand where a parent urgently demands an item and the son has no idea where to find it. Highlight meelTe's hyper-local search bar with live distance tags, showing that shoppers can pinpoint the closest stockist within seconds and complete the entire errand in 10 minutes.

---

## 2. Target audience

Young adults, sons, daughters, college students, and urban residents (18–35) living with family or running daily household errands; neighbourhood kirana, grocery, and general store merchants seeking immediate local footfall.
