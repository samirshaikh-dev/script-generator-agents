## 1. Concept & Overview
- **Title:** Maa Ka Order
- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Platform:** Vertical 9:16 (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, meelTe community feed)
- **Primary Goal:** Drive local walk-in footfall and promote meelTe's hyper-local search bar utility by highlighting everyday family problem-solving.
- **Audience:** Young adults, sons and daughters (18–35), urban families, and neighbourhood shoppers who handle urgent home errands.
- **Tone:** Warm, relatable, humorous, affectionate, triumphant.
- **Language:** Hinglish spoken dialogue with crisp English/Hinglish CTA.

## 2. Production Details
- **Setting:** Urban family home (living room & kitchen) + brief shot of neighbourhood kirana store counter.
- **Characters:**
  - Kabir (22), energetic son, eager to please but initially caught off-guard.
  - Mother (Sunita, 48), warm, bustling in kitchen making festive sweets/kheer.
  - Local Shopkeeper (friendly neighbourhood kirana owner).
- **Props:** Smartphone with meelTe installed, small box of saffron/kesar, cooking pot with ladle, wall clock.
