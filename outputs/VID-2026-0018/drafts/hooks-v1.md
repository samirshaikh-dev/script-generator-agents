# Hook Library & Strategy — VID-2026-0018

## 1. Strategy & Brief
- **Campaign / Request ID:** VID-2026-0018
- **Title:** Maa Ka Order
- **Objective:** Drive Local Discovery & Footfall (Objective 5 — Drive Participation) + Promote App Utility (Objective 1).
- **Target Audience:** Sons, daughters, young adults (18–35), and urban families tasked with quick household errands.
- **Participation Ladder:**
  - *Viewer:* Resonates with the sudden, high-stakes panic of a parent asking for an obscure or urgent household item.
  - *Participant:* Opens meelTe, types the item into "Search ads", checks the closest store's distance, and walks in.
  - *Regular Member:* Uses meelTe as their instant local shopping reflex for all household needs.
- **Tone & Voice:** Warm, humorous, heartwarming, family-centric, energetic.

---

## 2. Recommended Hook
- **Hook ID:** H07 (Category D — Relatable / User Verbatim)
- **Script line:** *"Beta, ek cheez leke aana, bahut zaroori hai." / "Yahan milega kahan?"*
- **Visual:** Mother peeking out from kitchen holding ladle, son on sofa freezing in sudden comic panic.
- **Psychological lever:** Instant universal family recognition, relatable errand anxiety, affectionate humour.
