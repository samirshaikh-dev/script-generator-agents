## 1. Video objective

**Promote meelTe App Features (Objective 1), Create Awareness (Objective 4), and Drive Local Participation (Objective 5).** Address the common consumer headache of discovery feed clutter when searching for critical essentials under time pressure. Showcase meelTe's hyper-local filtering mechanism (custom radius and category selection), proving that users can eliminate visual noise with two taps and immediately connect with nearby verified services like pharmacies.

---

## 2. Target audience

Urban smartphone users, working professionals, homemakers, caregivers, and families (20–45) who value speed, efficiency, and clarity when sourcing emergency supplies, daily necessities, or specialized local services.
