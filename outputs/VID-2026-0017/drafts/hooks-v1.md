# Hook Library & Strategy — VID-2026-0017

## 1. Strategy & Brief
- **Campaign / Request ID:** VID-2026-0017
- **Title:** Filter Magic
- **Objective:** Feature Education & Precision Search (Objective 1 — Promote App & Objective 5 — Drive Participation).
- **Target Audience:** Urban smartphone users, busy homemakers, young professionals, and caregivers (20–45) needing immediate local medical/daily essentials without clutter.
- **Participation Ladder:**
  - *Viewer:* Resonates with the frustration of endless irrelevant search results in an urgent moment.
  - *Participant:* Opens meelTe, taps Filters, selects specific distance and category, and finds nearby shops instantly.
  - *Regular Member:* Relies on meelTe as the go-to precision directory for all neighbourhood services and emergencies.
- **Tone & Voice:** Humorous, witty, relatable, problem-solving, plain-spoken.

## 2. Recommended Hook
- **Hook ID:** H05 (Category C — Problem-Based / User Verbatim)
- **Script line:** *"Mujhe dawai chahiye, gamla nahi."*
- **Visual:** Woman holding her head in comic distress, glaring at an endless stream of plant nursery photos on her phone.
- **Psychological lever:** Instant comedic contrast, visceral irritation with search clutter, and empathetic recognition.
