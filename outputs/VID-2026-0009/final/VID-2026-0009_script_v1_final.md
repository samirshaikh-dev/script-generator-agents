## 1. Video objective

**Build Connection, Build Trust & Drive Participation.** Celebrate solidarity among local merchants. Instead of viewing neighbouring shopkeepers as competitors, meelTe empowers business owners to introduce fellow merchants to the platform, earning mutual referral coins and growing the local market together.

---

## 2. Target audience

Local shopkeepers and retailers in traditional bazaars and neighbourhood commercial streets who discuss daily business over tea breaks, and value genuine peer recommendations.
