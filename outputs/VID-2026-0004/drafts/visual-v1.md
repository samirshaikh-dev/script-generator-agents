## Pacing & Editing Notes
- The 0-3s hook captures an everyday, immediate customer trigger ("Bhaiya, ye stock mein hai?").
- The 3-21s middle section moves with brisk, rhythmic momentum: Reply (6s) → Photo (6s) → Location (6s). Each action takes exactly 6 seconds, showcasing how seamless the 3-step interaction is.
- The 21-26s payoff provides real-world gratification: the customer physically walking in and buying within 10 minutes.
- The 26-30s end card gives 4 undisturbed seconds for the punchy 4-phrase CTA.
- Filmed in portrait (9:16) on a mobile phone in a local retail shop with natural ambient shop lighting.
