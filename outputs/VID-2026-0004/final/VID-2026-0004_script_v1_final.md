## 1. Video objective

**Build Trust, Build Connection & Drive Participation.** Eliminate the doubt and friction that stops neighbours from visiting local shops. By using meelTe chat to reply, send a live shelf photo, and share an exact location pin, local shopkeepers give customers 100% certainty, converting online chats into in-store visits within 10 minutes.

---

## 2. Target audience

Local retail shop owners (electronics, home goods, apparel, stationery, hardware) who receive frequent inquiries about stock availability, and neighbourhood shoppers who want fast, reliable confirmation before stepping out.

---

## 3. Hook (0–3s) — selected verbatim
