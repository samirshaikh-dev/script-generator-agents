- 8-15s provides rich, sensory tactile gratification—the physical joy of holding, verifying, and smiling in a local store.
- 15-20s leaves a full 5 seconds for the clean 4-part CTA and official end card.
- Filmed in 9:16 vertical video format across two locations (apartment doorway & neighbourhood lifestyle shop).
