## 1. Video objective

**Create Awareness (Objective 4), Build Trust (Objective 2), and Drive Participation (Objective 5).** Address the widespread consumer pain point of transit breakages and damaged online courier deliveries. Highlight that fragile, aesthetic, and tactile items (like ceramics, glassware, and home decor) are best experienced in person. By showing how meelTe connects shoppers with nearby stores displaying verified photos, prices, and address details, the script encourages direct footfall and sensory confidence.

---

## 2. Target audience

Urban consumers, homemakers, lifestyle buyers, and gift seekers (20–45) who value physical product quality and are tired of fragile goods arriving broken in courier transit; local neighbourhood boutique, crockery, and home decor store owners.
