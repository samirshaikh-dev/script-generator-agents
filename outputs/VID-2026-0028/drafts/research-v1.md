# Research Summary — VID-2026-0028

## Core Analysis
- **Goal**: Highlight user safety, boundary control, and peace of mind on meelTe by showing that unwanted interactions can be blocked effortlessly in one tap, keeping community networking safe and pleasant.
- **Audience**: Women, young adults, college students, and creators in India (Bengaluru, Ranchi, Gaya, etc.) who hesitate to join local social platforms out of fear of harassment, spam, or creepy stranger interactions.
- **Tone**: Relatable tension transitioning into deep emotional relief and self-assured calm. Warm, dignified, and empowering—never panic-inducing or dark.
- **Key Message**: Your safety and boundaries are absolute. On meelTe, you control who talks to you, and when you block someone, they vanish completely from your profile and feed.
- **House Screen Reference**: Clip E (Blocked Interests screen with shield icon and "You're all set" message).

## Cultural & Psychological Anchors
- **Cultural Reality**: Women on social media and dating/community apps frequently face boundary overstepping. The moment an app provides instant, total blocking with zero awkwardness, user trust skyrockets.
- **Sensory Relief Moment**: The transition from annoying haptic buzzes to calm purple UI and sipping a hot cup of chai conveys peace and total ownership over one's digital space.
- **Brand Voice Alignment**: meelTe is about positive real-world local community. Safety features are not an afterthought; they are the foundation that allows real connections to happen safely.

## Claims to Verify
- Exact phrasing on Clip E: "Blocked users can't contact you or see your profile" and "You're all set" with shield icon.
- No invented subscription, reporting tiers, or complex verification processes mentioned.
