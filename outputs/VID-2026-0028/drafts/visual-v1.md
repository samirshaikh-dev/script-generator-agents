# Visual Direction Draft — VID-2026-0028 (v1)

## Video Style & Pacing
- **Style**: Emotional micro-drama shifting rapidly from irritation/overwhelm to serene, tactile relief. High production value, cozy cafe aesthetic.
- **Pacing**: Staccato and vibrating from 0-3s, deliberate and decisive at 3-9s, smooth and serene from 9-20s.
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 Vertical (1080x1920)
- **Palette**: Cafe warm woods and amber tea tones contrasting with meelTe Royal Purple (`#743BAA`), Soft Lavender (`#F4EFFD`), and Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`).

## Audio & Sound Design

- **SFX & Foley**:
  - `0-3s`: Intrusive, harsh buzzing vibration on wooden table (*Bzz-bzz... Bzz-bzz...*). Distant cafe ambient chatter.
  - `3-9s`: Soft fingertip screen taps, subtle UI click on "Block".
  - `9s`: Sudden, dramatic cutoff of all harsh buzzing. A soft, soothing chime/whoosh as the purple shield screen blooms.
  - `9-15s`: Gentle, warm lo-fi acoustic guitar / soft Rhodes piano chords entering warmly.
  - `15-20s`: Soft ceramic clink of the cup, subtle breath of relief, warm and reassuring voiceover outro.
