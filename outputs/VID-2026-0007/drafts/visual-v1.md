## Pacing & Editing Notes
- 0-3s instantly hooks viewers with the universal, comical reality of vague Indian street directions.
- 3-7s shows the shopkeeper's matching exhaustion.
- 7-12s uses the playful rewind mechanic to pivot directly into the practical meelTe feature (Select Location & Pin drop).
- 12-17s provides the instant contrast: same street, zero calls, direct arrival.
- 17-21s seals the emotional connection between customer and merchant.
- 21-25s gives 4 undisturbed seconds for the memorable 3-phrase CTA.
- Filmed in portrait (9:16) on mobile phone in a real local lane and shop with natural sunlight.
