## 1. Video objective

**Build Trust, Create Awareness & Drive Participation.** Eliminate the universal headache of vague verbal landmarks in neighbourhood commerce. By teaching shopkeepers how to drop an exact map pin via meelTe's "Select Location", local stores become effortlessly navigable, helping customers reach the counter on the first try without frustrating phone calls.

---

## 2. Target audience

Local shopkeepers situated in residential colonies, dense gullies, or traditional market lanes where landmarks cause confusion, and neighbourhood shoppers who rely on their phones for navigation.
