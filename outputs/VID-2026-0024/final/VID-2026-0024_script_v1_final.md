## 1. Video objective

**Drive User Engagement, Exploration & Rewards Participation (Objective 5 — Drive Participation) and Promote meelTe Coins Economy (Objective 1).** Address the ubiquitous modern habit of spending hours scrolling on social media feeds with zero tangible benefit. Showcase meelTe's Coins rewards system (Clips C & D), demonstrating how exploring neighbourhood merchant listings and community events earns users valuable coins redeemable for vouchers, turning passive screen-time into real savings.

---

## 2. Target audience

College students, Gen Z, mobile creators, young working professionals, and budget-conscious youth (18–26) who spend considerable time on mobile devices and love micro-rewards, shopping vouchers, and gamified discovery.
