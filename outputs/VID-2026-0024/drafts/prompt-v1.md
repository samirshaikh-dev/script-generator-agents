## 1. Concept & Overview
- **Title:** Scrolling Pays Now?
- **Duration:** 25 seconds
- **Platform:** Vertical 9:16 (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, meelTe app feed)
- **Primary Goal:** Drive daily active engagement, reward local curiosity, and educate users on meelTe Coins and voucher redemptions.
- **Audience:** College students, Gen Z, budget-conscious youth, and young mobile users (18–26) who spend hours on their phones and appreciate micro-rewards.
- **Tone:** Eye-opening, upbeat, relatable, punchy, rewarding.
- **Language:** Hinglish spoken dialogue/VO with English brand CTA.

## 2. Production Details
- **Setting:** Student hostel room / bedroom; study desk with laptop, open notebook, coffee mug.
- **Characters:** Dev (20), student feeling guilty about endless mindless scrolling, transforming into an excited local explorer.
- **Props:** Smartphone with meelTe installed, study desk, textbooks.
