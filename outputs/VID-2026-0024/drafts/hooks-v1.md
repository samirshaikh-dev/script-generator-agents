# Hook Library & Strategy — VID-2026-0024

## 1. Strategy & Brief
- **Campaign / Request ID:** VID-2026-0024
- **Title:** Scrolling Pays Now?
- **Objective:** Drive Daily Active Participation & Rewards Engagement (Objective 5 — Drive Participation) + Promote App Coins Economy (Objective 1).
- **Target Audience:** Students, Gen Z, mobile creators, young working professionals, and bargain hunters (18–26) looking for real value from their screen time.
- **Participation Ladder:**
  - *Viewer:* Resonates with the universal guilt of wasting hours mindlessly scrolling social media for zero return.
  - *Participant:* Opens meelTe, discovers the Coins dashboard, explores neighbourhood shops, and watches their coin balance grow.
  - *Regular Member:* Explores local deals daily, accumulates coins, and redeems vouchers.
- **Tone & Voice:** Eye-opening, witty, energetic, rewarding, transparent.

---

## 2. Recommended Hook
- **Hook ID:** H05 (Category C / L — Problem & Comparison / User Verbatim)
- **Script line:** *A bored student scrolling a feed. Text: "Hours spent scrolling: 100. Money earned: ₹0."*
- **Visual:** Young student slumped at study desk flicking thumb mindlessly, pale screen reflecting in tired eyes.
- **Psychological lever:** Visceral screen-time guilt, stark numerical contrast, immediate desire for financial return.
