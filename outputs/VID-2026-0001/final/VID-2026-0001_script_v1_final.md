## 1. Video objective

**Drive Participation.** Give local shop owners a simple, repeatable way to tell neighbours exactly when a festival offer is live — so "sale chal rahi hai" becomes "10 se 9 tak."

---

## 2. Target audience

Local shop owners running festival or seasonal offers who announce a sale without saying when it ends, and the neighbours who want to know when to turn up.

---

## 3. Hook (0–3s) — selected verbatim

A shopkeeper in a shirt that says "Festival Sale" looks at the clock.

**Shopkeeper:** "Subah 10 se raat 9 tak, bas."

---

## 5. Transitions, visuals and on-screen text

| Time | Shot / angle | Expression / delivery | Transition | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Medium close on the "Festival Sale" shirt, tilt up to the wall clock, snap to camera | Matter-of-fact, slightly proud | Hard cut on the clock tick | "10 AM → 9 PM" |
| 3-7s | Medium behind-counter shot, phone in hand | Practical, confiding | Match cut: clock face → phone time field | "Timing pakki?" |
| 7-12s | Over-shoulder on the phone, close on the Start field | Deliberate, unhurried | Push in on the tap | "Start: 10:00 AM" |
| 12-17s | Tight insert on the End field, then a quick pull back | Decisive | Overlay wipe as countdown appears | "End: 9:00 PM" |
| 17-21s | Handheld medium, customers entering past the camera | Warm, pleased | Handheld rush into the shop | "Aapki sale kab se kab tak?" + countdown |
| 21-25s | Wide hold on shopfront with end card | VO only, clean | Clean hold on end card | "Set the timing. Meelte." |

**B-roll:** clock second hand, fingers on the counter, thumb tap, screen glow on his face, clock nearing 9, footsteps.

**Music:** low, warm, lightly playful — beat drops under the hook, lifts through the rush, clears completely for the CTA.

**Editing and pacing:** fast hook cut, measured middle while he sets the schedule, quick handheld burst for the rush, clean still frame for the CTA.

**Filming note:** phone on a window ledge, natural light, one location. These are written directions for a human to shoot — no footage is generated.

## 6. Call to action and ending

**CTA voiceover over the end card (25s, verbatim from the brief):**

> "Set the timing. Create urgency. Meelte."

**Ending:** clean hold on the shopfront with the clock just before 9; the CTA lands alone with no music under it.

**Engagement moment (17–21s):** on-screen question — "Aapki sale kab se kab tak?" — invites viewers to answer in the comments before the CTA.

## 13. Community-building objective

Viewers → participants → regular members: a shopkeeper who sets a clear window makes the neighbourhood sale something people can plan around. The engagement question invites viewers to state their own sale timing, turning a product demo into a shared local practice rather than an advertisement.
