## 1. Video Objective & Value Proposition

**meelTe App Promotion.** Ensure every viewer clearly understands:
1. **meelTe is our app.**
2. **It helps nearby people see local offers.**
3. **They can download it from the Play Store and App Store.**

---

## 2. Target Audience

Neighborhood retail shopkeepers with unsold stock who want local footfall, and nearby residents looking for neighborhood deals.

---

## 3. Hook (0–3s)

Open immediately on the spoken problem in everyday Hinglish (7 words):

> **Ramesh:** *"Arre! Saamaan pada-pada kharab ho raha hai!"*

Paired with a loud, relatable reaction looking at stacked, unsold fresh inventory (good stock, never spoiled).
