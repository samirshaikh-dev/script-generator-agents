## 1. Video objective

**Create Awareness, Build Trust & Drive Participation.** Educate local business owners on the importance of selecting the right category when creating advertisements on meelTe. High-intent neighbourhood customers use category filters to find exactly what they need, connecting the right buyer with the right local shopkeeper without clutter.

---

## 2. Target audience

Local shopkeepers across all retail categories (grocers, chemists, boutique owners, electronics, services) who want relevant footfall, and neighbourhood shoppers who want fast, filtered local discovery.

---

## 3. Hook (0–3s) — selected verbatim
