## 1. Video objective

**Drive Participation, Build Connection & Create Awareness.** Help local retail shopkeepers quickly liquidate lingering, slow-moving inventory. By posting a 2-day clearance advertisement on meelTe featuring an eye-catching discount badge and a definitive deadline, business owners alert nearby bargain shoppers, freeing up shelf space and unlocking working capital.

---

## 2. Target audience

Independent local retail shop owners (apparel, footwear, kitchenware, electronics, lifestyle goods) burdened with slow-moving clearance items, and neighbourhood shoppers searching for limited-time local bargains.

---

## 3. Hook (0–3s) — selected verbatim

A shelf with just a few unsold items and a worried owner: "Purana stock, kya karun?"
