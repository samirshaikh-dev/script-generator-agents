# Hook Library & Strategy — VID-2026-0026

## 1. Strategy & Brief
- **Campaign / Request ID:** VID-2026-0026
- **Title:** Host and Earn
- **Objective:** Empower Grassroots Community Organizers (Objective 6 — Community Connection) & Drive Rewards Engagement (Objective 1 — Promote App).
- **Target Audience:** Artists, tutors, workshop organizers, creative freelancers, and hobby leaders (20–35) looking to build an engaged local following.
- **Participation Ladder:**
  - *Viewer:* Resonates with the exhaustion and self-doubt of organizing creative meetups alone.
  - *Participant:* Lists their workshop on meelTe, gathers neighbours, and successfully hosts their event.
  - *Regular Member:* Hosts recurring community sessions, building both an authentic neighbourhood tribe and milestone rewards.
- **Tone & Voice:** Validating, inspiring, warm, appreciative, rewarding.

## 2. Recommended Hook
- **Hook ID:** H03 (Category C / B — Problem & Question / User Verbatim)
- **Script line:** *A girl planning a small art workshop. Text: "Hosting is hard work. Is it worth it?"*
- **Visual:** Young artist surrounded by paintbrushes and blank canvases, resting her chin on her hand in weary contemplation.
- **Psychological lever:** Organizer burnout empathy, vulnerable self-doubt, anticipation of meaningful payoff.
