## 1. Video objective

**Empower Local Community Hosts & Creative Organizers (Objective 6 — Community Connection & Objective 5 — Drive Participation) and Promote Rewards Program (Objective 1).** Address the emotional and physical exhaustion experienced by independent artists, tutors, and workshop leaders when organizing local meetups. Showcase meelTe's event creation workflow (Clip L) and host milestone rewards (Clip C), demonstrating that meelTe honors their hard work with both an engaged group of neighbours and tangible coin rewards upon event completion.

---

## 2. Target audience

Artists, creative workshop hosts, yoga instructors, hobby teachers, musicians, and community organizers (20–35) seeking local participants, appreciation, and tangible recognition for their efforts.
