## 1. Video objective

**Build Connection, Build Trust, Create Awareness & Drive Participation.** Reframe the perceived battle between giant corporate e-commerce platforms and local independent businesses. While corporate giants rely on distant, impersonal warehouses, local merchants hold the ultimate competitive moat: human proximity and neighbourhood trust. By sorting discovery strictly by distance, meelTe ensures local merchants win on home ground.

---

## 2. Target audience

Local retail business owners feeling overwhelmed by quick-commerce and corporate online giants, and community-conscious neighbourhood residents who want to support local merchants.
