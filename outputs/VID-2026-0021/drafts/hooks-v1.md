# Hook Library & Strategy — VID-2026-0021

## 1. Strategy & Brief
- **Campaign / Request ID:** VID-2026-0021
- **Title:** Weekend Plan Hai?
- **Objective:** Drive Local Event Participation & Community Engagement (Objective 5 — Drive Participation) + Promote App Utility (Objective 1).
- **Target Audience:** Gen Z, college students, roommates, young working professionals, and friend groups (18–30) battling weekend boredom.
- **Participation Ladder:**
  - *Viewer:* Recognizes the frustrating, silent weekend doom-scroll with friends on a living room couch.
  - *Participant:* Opens meelTe, taps the Events tab, searches nearby happenings tonight, and heads out with friends.
  - *Regular Member:* Turns to meelTe every Friday and Saturday as their first reflex for local weekend discovery.
- **Tone & Voice:** Energetic, youthful, stylish, witty, relatable.

## 2. Recommended Hook
- **Hook ID:** H07 (Category D — Relatable / User Verbatim)
- **Script line:** *Friends on a sofa, all scrolling, nobody talking: "Kya karein aaj?"*

- **Psychological lever:** Visceral recognition of social media isolation among friends, shared boredom, desire for spontaneity.
