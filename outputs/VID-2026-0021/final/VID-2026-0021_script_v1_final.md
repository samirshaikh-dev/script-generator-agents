## 1. Video objective

**Drive Local Event Participation & Weekend Social Engagement (Objective 5 — Drive Participation) and Promote meelTe App Features (Objective 1).** Address the ubiquitous weekend frustration among friend groups: gathering at home with the intention of going out, only to succumb to silent phone scrolling because nobody knows what to do or where to go. Highlight meelTe's "Events" tab as the instant cure for weekend boredom, showing that users can discover curated, hyper-local gatherings (like a rooftop jam tonight) within seconds and transform their night.

---

## 2. Target audience

Gen Z, college students, roommates, young working professionals, and friend circles (18–30) seeking spontaneous, affordable local weekend plans without booking weeks in advance or sitting in traffic.
