## 1. Video objective

**Create Awareness, Build Trust & Drive Participation.** Confront the core frustration of local retail: stocking high-quality merchandise that neighbours walk past every day without knowing it exists. By showing how easy it is to post a complete advertisement on meelTe (name, category, photo, location pin, price), local merchants turn foot traffic into walk-in counter sales.

---

## 2. Target audience

Local independent retail business owners (home goods, gift shops, electronics, apparel, kitchenware) who feel invisible to passersby, and neighbourhood residents who want quality local alternatives.

---

## 3. Hook (0–3s) — selected verbatim

A shop owner sits behind the counter. Customers walk past without looking in.

**Voiceover:** "Achha saaman. Par kisi ko pata hi nahi."
