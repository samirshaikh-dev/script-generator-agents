# Hook Library — VID-2026-0010

Topic: Your Shop Is Invisible (Transforming an Overlooked Shop into a Nearby Discovery Hub)
Duration: 30s
Audience: Local independent retail shopkeepers & neighbourhood shoppers

## Selected Hook (Verbatim Input)
- **H00:** A shop owner sits behind the counter. Customers walk past without looking in. Voiceover: "Achha saaman. Par kisi ko pata hi nahi."
  - *Context:* Looking from inside shop through glass entrance as busy street pedestrians walk past without glancing inside.
