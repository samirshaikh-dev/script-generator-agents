## 1. Concept & Overview
- **Title:** Host Your Own Event
- **Duration:** 30 seconds
- **Platform:** Vertical 9:16 (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, meelTe community feed)
- **Primary Goal:** Drive real-world community building, offline social connection, and adoption of meelTe's self-serve "Create Event" feature.
- **Audience:** Young adults, hobbyists, chess players, book clubs, fitness/run clubs, and community organizers (18–35) seeking local offline meetups.
- **Tone:** Inspiring, warm, social, empowering, approachable.
- **Language:** Hinglish spoken dialogue with crisp English CTA.
