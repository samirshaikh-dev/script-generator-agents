# Hook Library & Strategy — VID-2026-0020

## 1. Strategy & Brief
- **Campaign / Request ID:** VID-2026-0020
- **Title:** Host Your Own Event
- **Objective:** Drive Community Building & Offline Social Connection (Objective 5 — Drive Participation & Objective 6 — Community Building) + Feature Education (Objective 1).
- **Target Audience:** Urban youth, hobbyists, chess and board game lovers, book readers, runners, creative organizers, and community builders (18–35).
- **Participation Ladder:**
  - *Viewer:* Resonates with the secret wish to host a fun meetup, but fears nobody will turn up.
  - *Participant:* Opens meelTe, taps Create Event, fills in details, drops a pin, and launches a free public event.
  - *Regular Member:* Hosts or attends recurring community clubs (chess, cycling, books, tech, arts) across their neighbourhood.
- **Tone & Voice:** Warm, encouraging, social, empowering, plain-spoken.

---

## 2. Recommended Hook
- **Hook ID:** H07 (Category D — Relatable / User Verbatim)
- **Script line:** *"Mera ek chai-and-chess evening karne ka mann hai, par kaun aayega?"*
- **Visual:** Young man sitting by an empty chess board holding a cup of tea, expressing vulnerable self-doubt.
- **Psychological lever:** Relatable fear of rejection, secret desire for community, vulnerability.
