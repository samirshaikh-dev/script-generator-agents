# Hook Library & Strategy — VID-2026-0022

## 1. Strategy & Brief
- **Campaign / Request ID:** VID-2026-0022
- **Title:** The Notification That Changed My Weekend
- **Objective:** Drive Community Event Participation & Local Awareness (Objective 5 — Drive Participation & Objective 4 — Create Awareness).
- **Target Audience:** Urban professionals, solo dwellers, cyclists, fitness lovers, and young adults (20–38) seeking weekend vitality and real-world local community.
- **Participation Ladder:**
  - *Viewer:* Resonates with notification fatigue, but is intrigued by a meaningful, life-enhancing alert.
  - *Participant:* Enables meelTe notifications, spots a nearby weekend meetup (cycling, running, social), and attends.
  - *Regular Member:* Becomes an active, weekly participant in local neighbourhood interest groups.
- **Tone & Voice:** Refreshing, personal, inspiring, authentic, heartfelt.
