## 1. Video objective

**Drive Community Event Participation (Objective 5 — Drive Participation & Objective 6 — Community Connection), and Promote meelTe Event Alerts (Objective 1).** Tackle the widespread fatigue of meaningless digital notifications by showcasing the transformative power of a hyper-local event alert. Demonstrate how an understated meelTe notification ("New Event near you") can nudge an ordinary resident out of weekend lethargy and into an exhilarating Sunday morning community cycling ride with neighbours.
