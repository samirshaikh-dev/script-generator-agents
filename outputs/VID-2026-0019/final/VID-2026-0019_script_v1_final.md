## 1. Video objective

**Promote meelTe App Features (Objective 1), Create Awareness (Objective 4), and Drive Inter-City Community Discovery (Objective 5).** Address the widespread consumer anxiety of travelling to another city for an outstation wedding or trip without knowing where to find authentic local goods, clothing, or emergency necessities. Showcase meelTe's location switcher ("teleportation" feature), demonstrating that users can change their destination city from home, browse local markets in advance, and land completely prepared.

---

## 2. Target audience

Interstate travellers, destination wedding guests, festive shoppers, business commuters, tourists, and students (20–40) traveling between major and regional Indian cities (such as Bengaluru, Ranchi, and Gaya) who need reliable neighbourhood shopping beforehand.
