## 1. Concept & Overview
- **Title:** Teleport to Any City
- **Duration:** 25 seconds
- **Platform:** Vertical 9:16 (Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, meelTe app feed)
- **Primary Goal:** Educate users on meelTe's manual city location switcher ("teleporting" search radius) to explore neighbourhood stores in other cities before travelling.
- **Audience:** Wedding travellers, festive shoppers, business commuters, tourists, and students (20–40) traveling across Indian cities.
