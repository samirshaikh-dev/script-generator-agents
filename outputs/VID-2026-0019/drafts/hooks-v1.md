# Hook Library & Strategy — VID-2026-0019

## 1. Strategy & Brief
- **Campaign / Request ID:** VID-2026-0019
- **Title:** Teleport to Any City
- **Objective:** Feature Education & Multi-City Discovery (Objective 1 — Promote App & Objective 4 — Create Awareness).
- **Target Audience:** Travellers, wedding guests, business visitors, students, and interstate commuters (20–40) traveling to destinations like Gaya, Ranchi, or Bengaluru.
- **Participation Ladder:**
  - *Viewer:* Resonates with the travel anxiety of not knowing where to shop or find services in an unfamiliar city.
  - *Participant:* Opens meelTe, taps the location pin, changes destination city, and explores authentic local merchants in advance.
  - *Regular Member:* Uses meelTe as their primary travel shopping guide across all supported Indian towns and cities.
- **Tone & Voice:** Travel-savvy, confident, relatable, solution-oriented.

## 2. Recommended Hook
- **Hook ID:** H07 (Category D — Relatable / User Verbatim)
- **Script line:** *"Shaadi hai Gaya mein. Wahan kuch milega kahan?"*
- **Visual:** Young man packing an open suitcase in his bedroom, holding a folded kurta with a worried frown.
- **Psychological lever:** Outstation travel anxiety, wedding shopping stress, and universal packing dilemmas.
