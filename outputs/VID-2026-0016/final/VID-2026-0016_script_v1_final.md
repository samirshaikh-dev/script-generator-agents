## 1. Video objective

**Drive Local Discovery & Direct Footfall (Objective 5 — Drive Participation) and Create Awareness (Objective 4).** Tap into the visceral consumer frustration of stifling summer heat paired with shocking appliance price tags. Demonstrate that shoppers do not need to overpay online or wait days for courier delivery—massive, verified seasonal discounts (like 28% OFF ceiling fans) are active at neighbourhood electrical stores just minutes away. By showing how meelTe surfaces nearby merchant deals and saves them with a tap, the video prompts immediate walk-in footfall.

---

## 2. Target audience

Urban residents, apartment renters, bachelors, students, and family budget managers (22–42) enduring hot weather who need affordable, immediate home appliances without delivery friction; local neighbourhood electrical and appliance merchants looking to attract walk-in shoppers.

## 10. Top 5 hooks with psychological angles

1. **H05 (Problem-Based / Verbatim):** *"Fan ₹2,995?! Bhai, budget nahi hai."*
   - *Psychological angle:* Visceral recognition of summer heat distress combined with immediate budget empathy.
   - *Opening visual:* Man wiping sweat with towel under squeaking, slow fan, gaping at phone price.
2. **H03 (Question):** *"Dopahar ki garmi mein jab ceiling fan hawa chhod de... toh pehla khayal kya aata hai?"*
   - *Psychological angle:* Automatic answer reflex prompting viewers to share their relatable summer misery.

3. **H23 (Comparison):** *"Online sale ka wait karke 4 din pighalna hai, ya 20 minute mein sasta fan ghar lana hai?"*
   - *Psychological angle:* Stark contrast highlighting the painful delay of delivery versus immediate local walk-in relief.

4. **H25 (Myth-Busting):** *"Log sochte hain discount sirf delivery apps pe milta hai... jabki bagal ki shop 28% sasta de rahi hai!"*
   - *Psychological angle:* Cognitive correction reflex overturning the false assumption that local shops don't offer discounts.

5. **H17 (Direct-Address):** *"Agar aapke room ka fan bhi sirf awaaz kar raha hai, hawa nahi... yeh video dekho!"*
