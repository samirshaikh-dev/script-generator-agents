# Hook Library & Strategy — VID-2026-0016

## 1. Strategy & Brief
- **Campaign / Request ID:** VID-2026-0016
- **Title:** Discount Dhamaka
- **Objective:** Drive Local Discovery & Footfall (Objective 5 — Drive Participation) + Objective 4 (Create Awareness).
- **Target Audience:** Cost-conscious urban residents, renters, students, and family budget managers (22–42) facing summer heat or needing appliances right away without waiting days for delivery.
- **Participation Ladder:**
  - *Viewer:* Recognizes the painful dilemma of high appliance prices during peak heat.
  - *Participant:* Opens meelTe, taps heart to save an active local deal, and walks into the nearby store.
  - *Regular Member:* Uses meelTe first whenever searching for neighbourhood deals, discounts, and store walk-ins.
- **Tone & Voice:** Energetic, humorous, empathetic, grounded in neighbourhood reality.

---

## 2. Recommended Hook
- **Hook ID:** H05 (Category C — Problem-Based / User Verbatim)
- **Script line:** *"Fan ₹2,995?! Bhai, budget nahi hai."*
- **Visual:** Sweating man slumps under a creaking, barely-turning ceiling fan, staring at high retail prices on his smartphone in utter disbelief.
- **Psychological lever:** Instant problem recognition, visceral summer heat empathy, and financial validation.
