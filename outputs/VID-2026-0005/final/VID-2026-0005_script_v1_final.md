## 1. Video objective

**Drive Participation, Build Community & Build Trust.** Help local craftsmen, tailors, and artisans transform empty shops into bustling community hubs. By hosting a free public workshop on meelTe, business owners share their craft, connect with neighbours face-to-face, and convert learners into lifelong customers.

---

## 2. Target audience

Local tailors, artisans, boutique owners, repair specialists, and craftspeople looking for genuine footfall; neighbourhood residents who want to learn practical hands-on skills in their local area.

---
